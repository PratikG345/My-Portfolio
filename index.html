About

Built systems first. Now I read what they produce.

I spent my first year in tech inside the data, not just looking at it — designing schemas, configuring ERP workflows, and shipping backend systems. Now I'm applying that same structural thinking to analytics.

Across a web development internship, an ERP support role, and my current position in recruitment operations, I've repeatedly done the same job under different titles: take unstructured, messy information and turn it into something a business can act on — a report, a dashboard, a repeatable process.

I'm not approaching analytics as a career change from zero. I already understand relational data modeling, query logic, and how transactional systems generate the reports analysts consume — I'm now formalizing that into Excel, SQL, and Power BI proficiency through structured coursework and applied projects, not just theory.
